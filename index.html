Mapping Human Neurodegeneration
Through Danio rerio

NeuroCast Z provides a unified, AI-enhanced database translating human NDD-associated genes to zebrafish orthologs, linking them to quantifiable phenotypes, validated behavioral assays, and current peer-reviewed research — advancing ethical, cost-effective alternative-model science aligned with the 3Rs framework (Replace, Reduce, Refine).

Optical Transparency

Zebrafish larvae remain optically transparent through development, enabling non-invasive live imaging of neurons, Ca²⁺ dynamics, and protein aggregates without dissection or fixation.

87% Genetic Homology

High conservation of disease-relevant genes and pathways with humans makes zebrafish an evolutionarily valid model for studying neurodegeneration mechanisms and therapeutic interventions.

High-Throughput Screening

Small size, external fertilization, and rapid development allow large-scale drug screening in 96-well formats — accelerating compound discovery at a fraction of mammalian model costs.

3Rs Ethical Framework

Directly replaces and reduces mammalian animal use while refining experimental protocols — fully compliant with international ethical standards for alternative models in biomedical research.

3Rs Framework Alignment

Replace mammalian models · Reduce animal use by 10–100× · Refine protocols with non-invasive imaging — NeuroCast Z supports institutional compliance with international ethical guidelines (EU Directive 2010/63, ARRIVE 2.0, AAALAC standards).
